## Method and guardrails

- English finance lexicon; phrase matching, three-token negation scope, neutral dead zone, ticker-line context and promotional-noise suppression.
- Shift: >=3 comparable mentions in each window, mean-score delta >=0.35 or bullish/bearish direction flip (direction boundary ±0.15). Sources must occur in both windows and have successful status.
- Price-unconfirmed alert additionally requires fresh, approximately window-aligned Yahoo bars and an absolute move <=0.5% or opposite direction. Missing/stale/weekend prices suppress this alert; there is no claim of forecasting or proven price lead.
- Unusual observed volume: >=6 recent and >=3 baseline mentions, >=3x hourly mention rate on comparable sources. Search caps and changing coverage can create artifacts; inspect evidence before interpreting an alert.
- First run compares recent vs earlier lookback. Later runs can fall back to a non-overlapping snapshot from the preceding seven days with matching settings and available sources. Sparse evidence stays insufficient.
- No trading orders, notifications, model accuracy claims or backtested predictive performance. Not investment advice.
